# AURA — Continuous Story Design Notes

## Revised direction

The website is intentionally one uninterrupted product scene.

- The hero is not a separate static image anymore.
- The 240-frame animation begins immediately from the first viewport.
- The canvas remains sticky until the final CTA.
- All narrative copy is layered around the same product.
- Component annotations use animated SVG leader lines on desktop.
- Mobile swaps the leader-line diagram for compact, readable component cards while keeping the same continuous canvas sequence.

## Callout sequence

- Outer enclosure
- Acoustic driver
- Acoustic chamber
- Memory-foam seal

The callouts appear progressively and remain visible together in the final exploded state.

## Visual rule

The product always remains the visual anchor. Typography and annotations explain it; they never replace it.
